# 📢 Public data policy

This policy applies to public repositories and public data.

## Allowed

- game catalogs and puzzle banks;
- browser-tool icons and licenses;
- public market/news snapshots;

## Not allowed

- resumes, CVs, or employment records;
- identity documents or driver licenses;
- health, financial, tax, or legal records;
- personal screenshots;
- credentials, recovery codes, or tokens;
- GPS-tagged images;
- old backups or reconciliation snapshots;
- internal hostnames or home-directory paths.

## File rules

- simple lowercase kebab-case names;
- no dates or tool-generated names unless the date is the content;
- images must have no EXIF, GPS, XMP, ICC, comments, or text chunks;
- remove metadata from every image before committing.

Private personal data belongs in `private/journal`. See the [backup policy](repository/backup.md).
